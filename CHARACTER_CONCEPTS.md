# The Awakened — Character Concept Briefs

Written concept-art briefs for all 7 heroes, meant to be copy-pasted
into an image generator (Midjourney, DALL-E, etc.) or handed to an
illustrator. There's no image-generation tool in this project, so
these are text specs, not finished art — see `DESIGN.md` §9 for why,
and for how the current in-game 3D rig (built from primitives, not
these designs) relates to this brief.

**Shared style direction for every prompt**, in case you want to
prepend it: *"Stylized hero-game character concept art, Brawl
Stars/Overwatch quality, bold clean silhouette, large expressive eyes,
soft cartoon shading, clean line art, dynamic pose, modern streetwear
with subtle supernatural glow effects, mobile game character sheet,
full body, plain background."*

Each hero keeps the identity already established in the game's data
(`src/scene/heroCosmetics.ts`) — skin tone, eye color, hair color — so
concept art and the in-game version read as the same character.

## Inferna — Fire · Mage

**Personality:** Quick-tempered but controlled; treats her power like
a craft, not a tantrum. Confident, a little smug when she lands a hit.

**Hair & face:** Short, tousled dark auburn hair (`#5a2a1a`) with a
couple of spiky tufts catching the light like embers. Warm tan skin
(`#e0a878`), amber eyes (`#c2621a`). Small glowing ember-freckle mark
on one cheekbone.

**Outfit:** A cropped black bomber jacket with seams that glow like
cracked lava (orange stitch-lines, not solid trim) instead of a
robe — modern streetwear, not fantasy wizard. Fitted charcoal tank
top underneath, combat pants with a few soot scuffs, laced boots.
Fingerless gloves that glow faintly at the fingertips when she casts.

**Signature focus:** No staff — her Fire Bolt/Flame Wave channel
straight through her hands. A small ember-gem pendant on a chain as
her one piece of jewelry, doubling as a focus item.

**Palette:** Charcoal/black base, orange-red glow accents, warm skin
tones — keep the *clothing* neutral/dark so the fire glow is what pops.

**Pose direction:** Mid-cast, one hand raised with a small flame
curling around the fingers, weight on the back foot, confident smirk.

## Earth Guardian — Earth · Tank

**Personality:** Steady, protective, dependable-big-sibling energy.
Speaks less, stands more.

**Hair & face:** Dark brown buzzcut (`#3a2f1a`), deep brown skin
(`#8a5a3a`), moss-green eyes (`#4a6a3a`). A small embedded stone chip
near one temple, like a scar that calcified.

**Outfit:** A heavy reinforced hoodie (thick canvas, not cloth-cloth)
with a stone-textured chest panel — like body armor built into
streetwear rather than plate armor over it. Stone gauntlets/forearm
guards with faint glowing mineral veins (green-brown), sturdy cargo
pants, heavy work boots.

**Signature focus:** The stone gauntlets themselves — no separate
weapon. Fortify/Guardian's Watch read as the gauntlets pulsing and
throwing up a shield, not a spell gesture.

**Palette:** Earthy browns and mossy greens, broad silhouette (widest
shoulders of the roster), heaviest-looking of the 7 heroes.

**Pose direction:** Grounded wide stance, one gauntleted fist raised
defensively in front of an ally (implied off-frame), calm/unbothered
expression even mid-block.

## Water Healer — Water · Support

**Personality:** Warm, nurturing, the type who's already handing you
a water bottle before you knew you needed one.

**Hair & face:** Long deep-blue hair (`#1a4a6a`) in soft flowing
waves/side-locks, light tan skin (`#d0b090`), cyan eyes (`#3ab8c9`). A
tiny cyan teardrop-shaped mark under one eye.

**Outfit:** A cropped blue-and-white windbreaker, open over a fitted
top, with translucent ribbon-like fabric accents at the sleeves/hem
that move like water rather than cloth. Utility belt with a couple of
small canteens/vials. Comfortable trainers, not boots — she's mobile,
not armored.

**Signature focus:** A small ornate water flask/canteen at her hip as
her one "focus" prop — Restoring Current/Encouraging Current read as
her uncapping it and the water turning to light before it reaches the
target.

**Palette:** Blues and whites, soft and light rather than heavy —
contrast with Earth Guardian's bulk.

**Pose direction:** Mid-motion, one hand extended with a small glowing
water droplet forming above her palm, gentle reassuring smile.

## Spark Duelist — Spark · Brawler

**Personality:** Cocky, high-energy, always looking for the next
fight — the "let's go" teammate.

**Hair & face:** Bright electric-yellow hair (`#e8d84a`) styled as a
short mohawk/spiked crest, tan skin (`#c99060`), bright yellow eyes
(`#f0e060`). A small lightning-bolt-shaped scar across one cheek.

**Outfit:** Sleeveless tactical vest over a fitted compression shirt,
fingerless gloves with metal knuckle plating that sparks on impact,
athletic joggers, high-top sneakers. A chain/dog-tag necklace.

**Signature focus:** A pair of energy-charged gauntlet-batons (not a
sword) — Charged Slash/Chain Spark read as quick electric strikes, not
swordplay, matching a Brawler's up-close aggression.

**Palette:** Yellow/electric-white accents on a dark athletic base
(navy/black), so the spark glow reads clearly against it.

**Pose direction:** Aggressive forward-leaning fighting stance, fists
crackling with small arcs, smirking straight at the camera.

## Undead Assassin — Undead · Speedster

**Personality:** Quiet, calculating, rarely wastes a word or a
movement. Not evil — just efficient.

**Hair & face:** Near-black hair (`#1a1a22`), mostly hidden under a
hood; pale gray-toned skin (`#a8a0a8`), glowing green eyes
(`#4ae86a`). A thin scar through one eyebrow.

**Outfit:** A dark asymmetric-zip techwear jacket with a hood, built
for speed rather than protection — no bulk anywhere. A scarf/mask
covering the lower face, faint glowing green thread-patterns along the
seams. Sleek running shoes, not boots.

**Signature focus:** A single curved dagger with a bone-pale handle,
trailing a thin wisp of green mist — Execute reads as a precise,
already-finished strike rather than a flashy windup.

**Palette:** Black/dark gray base with glowing green accents — the
only bright color on this hero should be the eyes and the mist.

**Pose direction:** Low, coiled stance like a held breath, dagger
already drawn, head slightly tilted — reads as *about* to move, not
mid-swing.

## Charm Gunslinger — Charm · Gunslinger

**Personality:** Flirty, self-assured, treats every duel like she's
already won it.

**Hair & face:** Bright pink hair (`#ff5fc0`) in a swept ponytail with
a couple of sharp asymmetric spikes framing her face, warm tan skin
(`#e0b090`), magenta eyes (`#c2489e`) — mostly hidden behind a dark
visor, her signature accessory.

**Outfit:** A fitted asymmetric crop jacket in pink and gold, fingerless
gloves, gold chain jewelry, a thigh holster, ankle boots. Sharp,
idol-meets-gunslinger silhouette — not a cowboy duster.

**Signature focus:** An ornate pink-and-gold pistol with a
heart-shaped hammer detail — Quickdraw/Called Shot read as quick,
precise, theatrical draws.

**Palette:** Pink and gold, warm and bright — the most "glamorous"
palette on the roster, deliberately, since Charm is the odd one out
from the other 6 more combat-utilitarian looks.

**Pose direction:** One-hip cocky stance, pistol held loosely at her
side, visor catching a glint of light, playful smirk.

## Spirit Mage — Spirit · Mage

**Personality:** Serene, otherworldly calm — the kind of person who's
already made peace with dying once and isn't afraid of a second try.

**Hair & face:** Long silver-white hair (`#e8e8f0`) trailing slightly
as if in a breeze that isn't there, pale lavender-tinted skin
(`#d8c8d8`), violet eyes (`#a86ae0`). A small glowing violet rune mark
on her forehead.

**Outfit:** A long modern open coat (not a robe) in pale lavender and
white, with a few small floating spirit-charm talismans orbiting near
her shoulders and hem rather than hanging on strings. Fingerless
gloves, soft flowing trousers, simple flat shoes — she doesn't need to
move fast, the charms do the active work.

**Signature focus:** The floating charms themselves — Spirit Bolt/Soul
Siphon read as a charm briefly flaring and lancing energy at the
target, then returning to orbit.

**Palette:** Pale lavender, white, soft violet glow — the most
ethereal/desaturated palette on the roster, deliberately quieter than
Fire or Charm.

**Pose direction:** Still, calm stance, coat hem lifted slightly by an
unseen breeze, one or two charms visibly orbiting, faint knowing smile.
